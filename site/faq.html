FAQ

Asked andanswered.

What is Qalibre?

A trading app for QMS that lives in Telegram. Paste any token to see what it really is, then buy or sell in one tap at the best price available, from a wallet only you control.

Can Qalibre take my money?

No. Your wallet is owned by a key on your phone. Qalibre can only settle trades into your own wallet, within limits you set, and has no way to withdraw or send your funds anywhere.

What happens if Qalibre disappears?

Your wallet is a contract on QMS, not an account with us. With your backed-up key you can move everything from any QMS wallet, without Qalibre.

Is the safety check a guarantee?

No. It catches the common scams (honeypots, hidden taxes, dangerous creator powers) and tells you plainly. A token can still lose value, and every card says so.

How do I get a better price than swapping directly?

When another trader takes the other side in the same batch, you trade with each other at one fair price and skip the pool fee and price impact. When nobody does, you get the exchange’s exact price and pay Qalibre nothing.

Where does the liquidity come from?

Traders first: orders in the same batch are matched with each other. What remains is routed to QMS exchanges (today QWAP, the main one), which earn their usual fee. Qalibre holds no pools of its own.

Do I need QMS for gas?

No. Network costs are taken at cost from the trade itself, never above the maximum shown. Protecting or moving your own money is free.

Why is it on testnet?

Real money deserves an independent audit first. Everything works on QMS testnet today with test tokens that have no value. Mainnet follows the external audit.

Can I use it in groups?

Yes, as a guard. Add Qalibre to a group and it warns everyone when someone posts a scam token, and stays quiet otherwise. Trading happens only in your private chat.

Every trade, protected.
