Risk notice

Trading tokens is risky. Here is what that means, plainly.

Tokens can lose all their value

Prices of new and small tokens can fall to zero, quickly. Only trade what you can afford to lose.

Safety checks are not guarantees

Qalibre’s check catches common scams, but a token can pass and still be harmful or worthless. Its creator may change it after you buy.

Small pools move

In a small pool, your own trade can move the price. Qalibre shows the impact and asks you to confirm large ones.

Software can fail

Smart contracts and the networks they run on can have bugs or outages. Qalibre is on testnet until an external audit is complete.

Keys are yours to keep

If you lose your key and your PIN, nobody can recover your wallet, including us. Back up your key.
