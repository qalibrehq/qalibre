FAQ

Asked andanswered.

What is Qalibre?

A trading app for QMS that lives in Telegram. Paste any token to see what it really is, then buy or sell in one tap at the best price available, from a wallet only you control.

Can Qalibre take my money?

No. Your wallet is owned by a key on your phone. Qalibre can only settle trades into your own wallet, within limits you set, and has no way to withdraw or send your funds anywhere.

What happens if Qalibre disappears?

Your wallet is a contract on QMS, not an account with us. With your backed-up key, imported into MetaMask or any EVM wallet, you can withdraw everything yourself. The contract does not need us.

How do I back up my wallet?

In the app, open Wallet and tap Back up your key. Enter your PIN and your wallet key is shown once. Write it down or save it in a password manager, and never share it.

Can I use my wallet in MetaMask?

Yes. Import your backed-up key into MetaMask, Rabby or any wallet that supports EVM networks, and add the QMS network. That account is the owner of your Qalibre wallet: your funds sit in the wallet contract it controls, and only it can withdraw them. To move everything into MetaMask itself, connect that account on our recovery page and press Move. It needs a little QMS for the network fee, runs entirely in your browser, and involves no Qalibre server.

What if I lose my phone or change phones?

Nothing is lost. Your key is stored encrypted with your PIN in your own Telegram account, not on the phone. Sign in to Telegram on the new phone, open Qalibre, enter your PIN, and your wallet is back.

What if I forget my PIN?

Then only your backed-up key can open the wallet. We never see your PIN or your key, so we cannot reset either. That is what makes the wallet yours, and it is why the app asks you to back up your key right after you create it.

Is the safety check a guarantee?

No. It catches the common scams (honeypots, hidden taxes, dangerous creator powers) and tells you plainly. A token can still lose value, and every card says so.

How do I get a better price than swapping directly?

When another trader takes the other side in the same batch, you trade with each other at one fair price and skip the pool fee and price impact. When nobody does, you get the exchange’s exact price and pay Qalibre nothing.

Where does the liquidity come from?

Traders first: orders in the same batch are matched with each other. What remains is routed to exchange pools on QMS, which earn their usual fee. Qalibre holds no pools of its own.

Do I need QMS for gas?

No. Network costs are taken at cost from the trade itself, never above the maximum shown. Protecting or moving your own money is free.

Why is it on testnet?

Real money deserves an independent audit first. Everything works on QMS testnet today with test tokens that have no value. Mainnet follows the external audit.

What is Fast mode?

An option for active traders. With Fast mode on, you can buy any token that passes the safety check without approving each one, and sell anything back to QMS in one tap. Honeypots and hidden taxes are still blocked, every trade still settles at no worse than a direct swap, and nothing can be withdrawn without your PIN. Your daily QMS limit is the most it can ever spend, so choose one you are comfortable with. Turn it on or off in Wallet.

Can my AI agent trade for me?

Yes. Connect Claude or any agent that supports MCP with a key from /agent in the bot. It can check tokens and trade inside your wallet, but never withdraw, never buy a token you have not approved, and never spend more than its own small daily cap. Every trade it makes is announced to you, and you can revoke it with one tap. How to connect.

Can I use it in groups?

Yes, as a guard. Add Qalibre to a group and it warns everyone when someone posts a scam token, and stays quiet otherwise. Trading happens only in your private chat.
