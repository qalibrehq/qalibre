Roadmap

Built in the open,one milestone at a time.

What is live today, what we are building next, and what must be true before real money is involved.

  1. Live now on QMS testnet Built, verified and in use
    • Trading from Telegram chat and the app
    • A safety check on every token
    • Batch settlement with an on-chain price floor
    • Self-custody wallets with limits, and a recovery page
    • Fast mode for active traders
    • AI agents, with no-key connection for Claude
    • Group scam alerts, share cards and invites
  2. Next, in the coming weeks Sharper trading
    • Limit orders that match each other
    • Price charts on token cards
    • A public live stats page
    • Listing in Claude’s connector directory
  3. Then, in early 2027 The trading layer for QMS
    • Routing across every QMS exchange as they launch
    • A developer API so other apps can trade through Qalibre
    • Batch matching solved by QMS useful-work miners
    • Post-quantum signatures for wallets
  4. Before mainnet Real value, independently audited
    • An independent security audit, published in full
    • Multisig governance with hardware keys
    • Mainnet launch

Dates follow readiness, not the other way round. Each step ships when it is safe.
