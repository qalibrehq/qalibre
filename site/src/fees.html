Fees

You pay for a better price,or not at all.

Qalibre earns only when it gets you more than a direct swap would. Everything else is passed through at cost.

Qalibre20% of what we save you versus a direct swap, never more than 0.2% of the trade. If we don’t beat a direct swap, you pay Qalibre nothing.
Exchange poolThe pool’s usual fee (0.3% today), only on the part of your trade that goes to a pool, exactly as if you swapped there yourself.
NetworkThe real cost, shared across everyone in the batch, taken from the trade. Never above the maximum shown before you trade.
Your moneyTurning trading off, changing limits, approving tokens and withdrawing cost nothing.
InvitesInviters earn 20% of Qalibre’s fee on their friends’ trades for 90 days. It comes out of our fee; friends pay the same.
An example

A direct swap would pay 1,000 tokens. Matched in a batch, you get 1,004. Qalibre’s fee is 20% of the 4 extra: 0.8 tokens. You keep 1,003.2, still 3.2 more than going direct.
