Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026. We collect the least we can to run the service.

What we never see

Your wallet key and your PIN. The key is created and encrypted on your device and stored in your own Telegram cloud storage.

What we store

Your Telegram user ID and first name, your wallet address, your orders and their results, invite relationships, and an encrypted trading key that can only trade within your limits. Group chats that add the bot are stored by ID so alerts can be turned off.

What is public

Your wallet address and its transactions are on the QMS blockchain, as with any blockchain wallet. Cards you choose to share show your first name and Telegram photo.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell data, show ads or track you across other sites. This website sets no cookies.

Deleting your data

Message the bot or email hello@qalibre.app and we will delete what we hold off-chain. Blockchain records cannot be deleted by anyone.
