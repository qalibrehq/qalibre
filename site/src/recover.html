Recovery

Your money, out.No Qalibre needed.

Connect the account that owns your Qalibre wallet and move everything into it. This page talks only to your wallet and the QMS network, never to our servers, and it never asks for your key.

  1. 1Connect the owner account

    Use MetaMask, Rabby or any wallet where you imported the key you backed up in Qalibre.

    No wallet found in this browser. On a phone, open this page inside your wallet app’s browser: open in MetaMask.

    Owner account
  2. 2Find your Qalibre wallet
    Qalibre wallet

    Found automatically from your owner account.

    Use a different wallet address
  3. 3Move your funds

    This account needs a little QMS to pay the network fee. On testnet you can get some free from the QMS faucet.

      Each transfer is a transaction you approve in your wallet. Funds go only to the owner account above; the wallet contract allows nothing else.

    Before you start

    1. Back up your key. In Qalibre, open Wallet, tap Back up your key, and enter your PIN.
    2. Import it. In MetaMask: account menu, Add account, Import account, then paste the key.
    3. Connect here. Your Qalibre wallet is found automatically and its balances appear.

    Never paste your key into a website, including this one. It belongs only in your wallet app.
